Privacy Policy

KoalaTeaOtter LLC — this website

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

This privacy policy covers the KoalaTeaOtter LLC website (this page and its siblings). It does not cover individual apps — each app has its own privacy policy linked from its product page.

The short version: this website does not track you, does not run ads, and does not collect any information unless you voluntarily reach out through the contact form.

Information We Collect

This website does not collect personal information on its own. The only way any information is submitted is if you choose to use the contact form. If you do, that form is powered by Google Forms and may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you write

Filling out the form is voluntary.

How We Use Contact Information

If you submit the contact form, we use your information only to respond to your message. We do not add you to any mailing list, share your information with anyone, or use it for any other purpose.

What We Don't Collect

This website does not use:

  • Analytics or visitor tracking of any kind
  • Advertising networks or tracking pixels
  • Cookies beyond what your browser sets by default
  • Any form of fingerprinting or behavioral tracking

Google Forms

The contact form on this site is hosted by Google Forms. When you submit a message, it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We receive the form submission in our Google account and use it only to reply to you.

Our Apps

Each app published by KoalaTeaOtter LLC has its own privacy policy. For DragonTrove, please see the DragonTrove Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Changes will only ever reflect what this website actually does — we're not planning to add tracking or ads.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Send us a message.
